The Problem

Millions of farmers.
Almost nobody to ask.

Over 70% of Nigeria's rural households depend on smallholder farming. But when a crop turns yellow, pests attack, or prices crash — most farmers have no expert to call.

1 : 10,000+

Too few extension officers

One government extension officer can serve thousands of farmers. Most smallholders may go a whole season without expert advice — problems get diagnosed too late, or never.

Up to 40%

Harvests lost to pests & disease

Pests like fall armyworm and diseases like blight and rust can destroy a large share of a season's harvest — losses that timely, correct advice could prevent.

Guesswork

Wrong inputs, wasted money

Without guidance, farmers buy the wrong fertilizer, spray the wrong chemical, or plant at the wrong time. Scarce cash is spent on inputs that don't solve the real problem.

And language matters. Most farming information online is in English, written for other climates and other crops. A farmer in Kano needs answers about dawa, gero, masara and wake — in the language they think in.

How It Works

Three steps — watch a real journey unfold.

Below, follow Musa — a maize farmer near Kano — through one complete Gonaaaa conversation. It plays by itself: from a sick leaf, to a diagnosis in Hausa, to the exact input he needs and a supplier 2 km away.

1

Ask or snap

Type a question, speak it in Hausa, or take a photo of the sick crop, pest, or animal. No forms, no menus — asking for help takes seconds, even for farmers who don't read.

2

Get a clear answer

In seconds, Gonaaaa identifies the likely problem and gives simple steps you can take today — cheapest effective methods first, chemicals only when truly needed, with safety guidance.

3

Get what you need

If the fix needs an input — seed, fertilizer, spray, storage bags — Gonaaaa names it and connects you to a verified supplier nearby with it in stock. One tap from advice to solution.
